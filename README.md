A one-image summary of the world's news of Sunday, 22 November 2026,
drawn as a modern magazine cover: a dark navy cover, a crimson lead panel
and five coloured news panels.

**Lead story:** Mexico captures drug lord 'El Chapo Isidro'.

**Also inside:** Alcaraz v Zverev for the ATP crown · allies press Israel to
open Gaza aid as floods hit · a fourth weekend of cost-of-living protests in
France · Herzog taps Eisenkot to form a government in Israel · France reach
the Nations Championship final against South Africa.

## How it works

`build_entry.py` draws `entry.png` (1024×1024, PIL + system DejaVu fonts,
no network). `main.py` (Python 3.13 standard library only) validates the PNG
(≤1024×1024, ≤1 MiB, complete IEND chunk) and copies it to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, deterministically — the same bytes every run.
